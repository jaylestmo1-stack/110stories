## 2026-06-03 - Navigation Anchors for Emoji Headers
**Learning:** GitHub's automatic anchor generation for headers with emojis often excludes the emoji or includes it in a specific way that requires exact matching (e.g., `#-content-warning` for `## ⚠️ Content Warning`). Including a "Quick Links" section at the top of long documents significantly improves the "Scent of Information" and accessibility for keyboard/screen reader users.
**Action:** Always verify generated anchor links for headers with emojis by checking the rendered HTML or following established patterns for the platform.
