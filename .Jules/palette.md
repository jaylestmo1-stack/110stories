## 2026-07-02 - Compassionate UX for Archival Discovery
**Learning:** For traumatic archives, the "README-as-UI" pattern remains most effective when balancing transparency (Content Warnings) with scannability (Visual Cues). Using representative icons in perspective lists (e.g., 🚒, 🏙️) reduces cognitive load for researchers seeking specific narratives, while "Engagement Choice" links (Preview vs. Download) provide essential agency before encountering sensitive content.
**Action:** Use scannable icon-bulleted lists for perspective discovery and maintain dual-access links with explicit ARIA labels.
