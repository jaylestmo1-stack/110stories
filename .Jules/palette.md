## 2026-03-15 to 2026-03-17 - Interactive Crisis & Keyboard UX
**Learning:** Sensitive digital archives benefit from direct `tel:`/`sms:` URIs, semantic `<nav>` landmark navigation, consistent headers, and `accesskey` shortcuts with `<kbd>` hints for power and assistive users.

## 2026-03-20 - Platform-Agnostic Shortcut UX in ARIA Labels
**Learning:** Hardcoding platform-specific modifiers (e.g., `Alt+`) in ARIA labels for `accesskey` can confuse users on other OSs. Keeping ARIA labels generic (e.g., `Shortcut: A`) while visually documenting dual-platform legends optimizes accessibility.
**Action:** Use generic key names in ARIA labels and provide clear dual-platform visual legends.
