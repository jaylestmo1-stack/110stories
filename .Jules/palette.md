## 2026-05-15 - README-as-UI Pattern for Content Archives
**Learning:** In document-centric repositories, the README acts as the primary interface. UX is significantly improved by prioritizing emotional safety (Content Warning) and reducing interaction cost through 'Scent of Information' (Themes and metadata).
**Action:** Use metadata tables (Format, Size, Pages) and qualitative lists (featured voices) to help users make informed download decisions.
