## 2026-03-14 - Inclusive Archival Trust & Multi-Platform UX
**Learning:** Digital archives benefit from high-density "README-as-UI" with immediate crisis support, explicit metadata (verified dates, short SHA-256), and multi-platform keyboard hints (e.g., `<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>`) to minimize friction and build trust.
**Action:** Use scannable, multi-platform hints and trust indicators in sensitive static archives.
