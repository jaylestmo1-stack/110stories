## 2026-06-06 - Emotional Hooks and Constraint Management

**Learning:** Adding a representative quote from a narrative collection near the top of the README creates an immediate "emotional hook," humanizing the data and providing instant context for the user. Additionally, the 50-line PR constraint for Palette applies to the total diff size; consolidating sections and removing non-essential technical snippets (like bulky BibTeX blocks) helps maintain a compact, high-impact README-as-UI.

**Action:** Look for evocative content to feature as an introduction in document-centric repos. Be aggressive in pruning secondary technical documentation (citations, etc.) to prioritize primary UX elements within strict line limits.
