## 2025-05-24 - Content Warning for Sensitive Archives
**Learning:** For repositories containing sensitive or potentially triggering historical content (like 9/11 narratives), providing a clear but non-intrusive content warning is a critical accessibility and UX step. It allows users to prepare themselves emotionally before engaging with the material.
**Action:** Always include a "Content Warning" or "Sensitive Material" section in the README of archives dealing with traumatic historical events.
