## 2026-07-20 - UX Patterns for Sensitive Digital Archives & Immediate Utility
**Learning:** High-impact digital archives benefit from "README-as-UI" with "Engagement Choice" access, "Compassionate Navigation", and high-density support resources (like 988) to reduce user distress. Providing explicit document metadata (searchability, pages, read time, keyboard shortcuts) within the primary call-to-action reduces cognitive friction and interaction cost.
**Action:** Use scannable, accessible interfaces with immediate support pathways and embedded asset metadata in download link attributes.

## 2026-07-25 - 24/7 Reassurance & Regional Clarity
**Learning:** Explicitly stating "24/7" availability and regional coverage (e.g., "US & Canada") for critical support resources like the 988 Lifeline provides immediate reassurance and reduces the user's burden of verification during moments of distress.
**Action:** Always include availability and regional context for crisis intervention links.
