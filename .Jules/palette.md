## 2026-03-15 to 2026-03-20 - Archive & Platform-Agnostic UX
**Learning:** Sensitive archives benefit from direct `tel:`/`sms:` URIs, semantic `<nav>` landmarks, consistent headers, and `accesskey` shortcuts. Avoid hardcoding platform-specific modifiers (like `Alt+`) in ARIA labels; keep them generic (e.g., `Shortcut: A`) and document visual dual-platform legends.
**Action:** Use semantic wrappers, mobile-responsive triggers, generic ARIA shortcut labels, and dual-platform visual legends.

## 2026-03-23 - Interactive Support Accessibility & Multidisciplinary Citations
**Learning:** Screen readers reading out-of-context text like "Call" or "text" for crisis links benefit from descriptive `aria-label`s. Researchers and students benefit immensely from immediate MLA/APA citations alongside BibTeX.
**Action:** Provide descriptive aria-labels on short action links and offer both plain-text (APA/MLA) and code-based (BibTeX) citations.

## 2026-03-24 - Content Warning Promotion and International Support Discovery
**Learning:** Content warnings are critical for emotional safety and are highly enhanced by wrapping them in GitHub-native callout blockquotes (`[!WARNING]`), which provides immediate visual hierarchy. International users are better served with highly visible internationalization support signifiers, such as action-oriented links with semantic icons (e.g. `🌐`).
**Action:** Wrap critical warnings in native callouts and ensure all global support mechanisms have descriptive visual icons and distinct, accessible links.
