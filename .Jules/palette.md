## 2026-07-04 - UX Patterns for Digital Archives
**Learning:** For traumatic archives, the "README-as-UI" pattern is most effective when it balances scannability (icons, metadata) with accessibility (ARIA, keyboard shortcuts). Providing "Engagement Choice" links and explicit usage metadata improves trust and agency.
**Action:** Use scannable, accessible, and transparent patterns for archive interfaces.

## 2026-07-05 - Compassionate Navigation
**Learning:** Users engaging with traumatic content benefit from immediate support pathways located within content warnings. This reduces emotional friction by providing an "escape hatch" before or during engagement.
**Action:** Link Content Warnings directly to Support sections for immediate assistance.
