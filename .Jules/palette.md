## 2026-04-09 - Upgrading Critical Warning Inline Anchors for Non-Ambiguous Audio Assistance
**Learning:** Distressed or visually impaired users landing on a traumatic archive depend entirely on screen-readers to parse content warnings. While text links are standard, inline markdown shortcuts like `[view support resources below](#support)` lack descriptive audio announcements, causing screen-reader disorientation. Converting them to semantic HTML anchor elements with explicit `aria-label` and `title` tooltips ensures precise, unambiguous verbal rendering and visual confirmation without scrolling friction.
**Action:** Always upgrade inline warnings or navigational links to semantic anchor tags with descriptive `aria-label` attributes and hover `title` cues.
